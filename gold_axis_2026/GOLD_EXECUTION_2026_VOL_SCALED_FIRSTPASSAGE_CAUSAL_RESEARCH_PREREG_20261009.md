# 2026-10-09 prereg: volatility-normalized observed close first-passage to remove false 2026 regime shift

This is a **NEW EVENT DEFINITION** (not directional model retraining), and cannot be equated to 2025 or 2026 UP/DOWN overnight endpoint accuracy. Research based on López de Prado, Advances in Financial Machine Learning (2018) chapter3, triple-barrier volatility-scaled horizontal first hits, and ARCH conditional time-varying variance (Engle 1982, Econometrica doi:10.2307/1912773).

Original first-passage had FIXED ±0.5% price thresholds; first-passage hit incidence jumped 2023 55.56%, 2025 80.81%, 2026 mirror 95.28%, perhaps a trivial volatility scaling artifact. 2026 conditional distribution already seen many times; this is inspected retrospective.

Source sets from exact 2020–2025 EV Dukascopy + 2026 upstream-mirror Jan–Aug20 and primary-native M15 through Oct7. Source-qualified only Mon–Thu overnight 17TR→next09TR and source-complete observed BID 15min closes (original gate from firstpassage diagnostic); Friday64h never rebranded 16h. At each date 17TR, calculate *ex ante overnight RMS* sqrt(mean(prev21 fully matured normal eligible overnight log-returns²)). Min21 prior verified normal overnight source events. Thresholds fixed +1*RMS and -1*RMS in LOG space, no retrospective regime-adjustments, no price future lookahead. First crossing of observed 15m close => UP_FIRST, DOWN_FIRST, neither=>NO_HIT. Compare same exact dates with former FIXED ±0.5% barriers, report hit-rate, class mix, first-hit vs endpoint disagreement and preceding RMS distribution by year/source.

To separate volatility-scaled label shift from real predictive information, score two SAME labels baselines for each definition: strict previous-calendar-year event frequencies (prior matured only and frozen at Jan1) and last63 completed normal overnight event frequencies with Dirichlet α=1 per class, continually updated AFTER event maturity (not current day). Primary descriptive diagnostics: 2026 vs2025 class distribution Jensen-Shannon distance and year-by-year class incidence; rolling63 and last-year Brier on dynamic labels, no retrospective tuning. DO NOT interpret cross-label Brier difference as an improvement in signed endpoint direction. Exact source and date cohorts mirror vs native differ, not independent.

No lookahead, no period-overlap, no price stop/limit fill, no real Turkish bank spreads, no deployment. If rolling21 RMS creates variable barriers but 2026 still changes greatly, report not a silver bullet.
