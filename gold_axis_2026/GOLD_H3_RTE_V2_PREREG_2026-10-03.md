## 1. Motivation from RTE V1 DEV only

RTE V1 failed the 2023-2024 admission gate. A DEV-only false-positive diagnostic compared high-tension true rescues with broken flips.

The dominant separation was not higher acceleration. It was the opposite:
- true rescues had high accumulated tension but near-flat current change;
- broken calls were disproportionately generated by sharp one-origin jumps in `p_rte` / tension.

At V1 threshold 0.75:
- median `dp_rte` rescued ≈ 0.004
- median `dp_rte` broken ≈ 0.175

This motivates a distinct mechanism:
> reversal transition may be a persistent slow-burn state, while many false alarms are transient tension spikes.

No 2025 or 2026 outcome was inspected to create this hypothesis.

## 3. Sequential slow-burn state

For every eligible V5-continuation origin:
- `prev_p_rte` = previous eligible RTE probability
- previous origin must have the same momentum direction
- feature-cutoff gap must be <= 5 calendar days
- `dp_rte = p_rte - prev_p_rte`

Persistent-state requirement:
- `prev_p_rte >= 0.60`

Spike veto:
- `dp_rte <= 0.05`

These constants are frozen from the V1 DEV mechanism diagnosis before V2 evaluation.
