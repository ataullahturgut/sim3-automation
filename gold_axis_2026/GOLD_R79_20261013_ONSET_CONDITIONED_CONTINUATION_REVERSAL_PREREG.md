# R79 preregistration — CONDITIONAL gold move continuation vs reversal **after onset**
Date: 2026-10-13, written before selecting any first-move qualifying event or seeing outcome direction.
User objective: monthly trend drives primary gold vs cash, but SHORT corrective / opportunistic gold reallocations can be decided **AFTER a move has visibly started**, not only predicted before start.
**Scientific contrast:** R76–78 day09→3d horizon sought pre-onset first-touch shocks. R79 freezes a *different* answer: given an already observed M15 09→t move and original independent asof features, does the SAME SIGN later continue or reverse? This may be easier than pre-onset risk but not inherently profitable.

## Origin and label
ONE earliest 15-minute first threshold crossing per calendar TR weekday, between 10:00–16:00 local **bar-end**. Fix signal onset magnitude `abs(log(mid_M15_CLOSE / mid_09:00_OPEN)) >=0.0075` (75bp = material vs 25bp bank-cost proxy but never an actual bank spread). Event sign = sign of first qualifying completed bar. Source 2020–25 EV/Dukascopy M15, 2026 direct Dukascopy M1-matched15; only valid full continuous issue-day source; 09 anchor M15 OPEN at 09:00. Critical: signal is only known AFTER 15-min bar is complete. "Issue" = `bar_start+15min` TR and an actual price observed at issue has no verified bank ASK/BID quote. No looking at intrabar high/low of future bars to trigger earlier.
- Post-decision ending time same-day 16:30TR: M15 bar starting 16:30 OPEN (future to trigger time). Record continuous remaining `log(P16:30 / Psignal_close)`, signed by onset sign, and **same-direction continuation** binary if signed return >0, **reverse** if <0 (zero = non directional). Secondary economically consequential movement: `log(P16:30/Psignal) >= +0.005` in onset sign as continuation>=50bp; <=-0.005 as reversal>=50bp; neither, and future max/min excursion after onset up to16:30 (not known at issue). This is NOT R77 first touch. Also next future trading09TR price after overnight, separate and optional, no mixing later labels. No claims of guaranteed future return, and no 'hold to 16:30' if bank 16:30 price cannot execute.
- Minimum >=60 complete source bars on issue-day 00→16:30, no future gaps >15m inside observed signal09→onset; reject source-vintage crossover and weekend source gaps. 2023 HistData insufficient irrelevant: use EV full 2023–25, 2026 direct source.

## Baselines, predictors and tests
One fixed threshold75bp, not tested across many choices. Separate UP-onset and DOWN-onset branches. Predecision predictors: onset sign, magnitude at closed event bar, minutes since09 and until16:30, past 1h 15-min signed returns/median RV computed from bars ending <=issue; preceding completed 09→09 return and preissue 4h 05→09 realized volatility only if source-complete; optional macro3 at08:45 only for matched subset in later phase, not required pilot.
Stage1 before model: prevalence by year 2023,2024 DEV vs 2025,2026 opened, net post-onset median signed returns; fraction true continuation/ reversal and +/-50bp events. Source quality and sample counts.
Stage2: low-complexity L2 regularized logistic target: positive sign future 16:30 return, and separately adverse-reversal>=50bp probability. Fit strictly previous years matured event observations; DEV 2023 using train 2020–22; 2024 2020–23; freeze 2025–26 from 2020–24. Compare **on same rows** (a) frozen preyear class prevalence + rolling matured last60 and (b) naive unconditional onset-sign persistence (p=.5 net conditional). Chronological training standardization only. Brier, AUC, balanced accuracy, event separation, top-quintile false-exit vs avoided drawdown, same-date precision, bootstrapped paired intervals.
**Critical acceptance:** distinguish genuinely actionable **post-trigger** more than 50bp and bank spread costs; must beat rolling60 Brier in 2023/24 and at least 2025/26 individually and paired CI excluding zero with enough coverage, AND matching monthly asof if actual allocation performance is claimed. No threshold optimized based on 2026. Avoid overlapping trigger event strategies; at most one event per calendar day.
