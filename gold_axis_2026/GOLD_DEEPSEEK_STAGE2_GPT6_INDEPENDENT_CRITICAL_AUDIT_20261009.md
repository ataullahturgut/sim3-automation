## Key technical corrections to DeepSeek
1. **Impossible in-sample training dates.** DeepSeek's purported `beta` estimated on 2020–22 GC observations cannot be fit from the currently certified acquired **2023–2026** CME GC archive. The known 2020–22 XAU source is not a 2020–22 GC source. Thus the proposed specific beta residual is BLOCKED as formulated. Re-anchoring on 2023/24 would be a **different pre-registered** retrospective experiment with sparse eligible pairs, not a validated fix.
2. **Residual orthogonality is mischaracterized.** Regressing `r_XAU` on `r_GC` produces an **in-sample residual orthogonal to the GC regressor** under OLS assumptions; it does **not** independently make that residual orthogonal to `r_XAU`, nor prove that GC leads spot. The re-expression `z = r_XAU - beta*r_GC` contains price information from both feeds. Incremental information requires controlling the original XAU return on the **identical matched dates**; simple partialling-out is not discovery evidence.
3. **The study's exact hourly boundaries are not intrinsically leaking:** An H1 bar labeled **15:00** Europe/Istanbul is interval 15:00–16:00 and can be marked known by 16:15, well before a 16:45 decision. Our study used 14h and 15h GC starts, matching spot 15h/16h closed prices; zero post-17 overlap. DeepSeek's generalized statement that an H1 source “may overlap” is a QC warning, not a proven leak in this executed comparison.
4. **The source gate need not demand every possible date:** A legitimate *population-conditional* forecast can evaluate exact eligible, complete cases with same-date controls, but may not generalize to a broader population. Nonrandom coverage must be reported (observed above). Retrospectively dropping dates after observing labels, or imputing missing GC as zero, is forbidden.
5. **A sign-flipped predictor is a symmetric complement:** For binary unconditional decisions, replacing every label by its opposite makes BA exactly 100%-original BA (absent abstains). One must instead assess a sign orientation frozen in genuine development and preserved in later samples. Merely saying flipped sign is “comparable” is not a rigorous independent null test.
6. **Forecast target identity:** DeepSeek's proposed DAY label was correct but its candidate prior-session covariate and GC fitting date must be audited against 08:45; OVN 17→next09 remains the more relevant separately defined objective. No after-17 surprise values may enter the overnight decision. A macro schedule is at best advance-known exposure, not signed realized shock.
