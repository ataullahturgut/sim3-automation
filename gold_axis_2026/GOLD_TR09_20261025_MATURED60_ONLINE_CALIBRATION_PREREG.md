# TR09 H3 — 60 matured-label online base-rate recalibration (preregistered 2026-10-25)

**State:** preregistered, no outcome scoring executed yet. Research-only. Branch HEAD before this study: `8f81977875e4be99b7b24147333d2885c1ed6fb9`; canonical `gold_axis_2026/GOLD_INTRAMONTH_OPPORTUNITY_PROJECT_MANIFEST.md` blob `279dae0b2d5694593adda076d644a5d186241304` read before this write; both preserved. This is NOT a new static lagged-price, drawdown, calendar, or volatility gate: it tests whether **prequential adaptation to labels already matured before the forecast** mitigates 2025/2026 tail-base-rate drift.

## Fixed hypothesis, temporal contract
For an origin at 09:00 Europe/Istanbul, classify the direction and >=2% downside risk of XAU/USD from that origin's 09:00 reference quote to the 09:00 quote of the **third NEXT eligible business date**. Historical 09:00 reference = completed 08:45–09:00 local M15 `close_price` in `public.gold_research_histdata_xau15m_candidate`, `native_minute_bars=15`. Historical vendor firstprint and zero-latency execution are NOT verified: all results strictly retrospective, NOT tradable PIT certification. Data retrieved 2026-10-08. Explicit closed dates 2023-01-02/04-07/12-25, 2024-01-01/03-29/12-25, 2025-01-01/04-18/12-25, 2026-01-01/04-03; 2026-09-25 quote missing and NOT a holiday. Require all five prior 09 quotes, origin quote and target quote, never bridge missing anchors. No future target prices are predictors.

Train origins 2023–2024, **only** if target_date<=2024-12-31. Use all qualifying training rows N expected 496 to freeze train base UP and tail probabilities, 5-session vol training Q75, and all comparator gates. Test 2025 and 2026 Jan–Sep separately, equal rows. NEW online calibrator at each test origin d uses **only the latest 60 eligible historical rows whose target_date<d**, in chronological origin order; rows can be from training or earlier test years, but every incorporated label must have fully matured strictly before d. Let n be available matured rows (<=60), k_up and k_tail corresponding successes. With fixed 20 pseudo-observations at frozen training rate, p_online_up=(k_up+20*p_train_up)/(n+20), p_online_tail=(k_tail+20*p_train_tail)/(n+20). UP iff p_online_up>=0.5. The 60 and 20 are frozen a priori, not tuned to 2025/2026.

Comparators on EXACT SAME 2025/26 dates and train: frozen constant Laplace; frozen two consecutive UP; frozen 5-session vol Q75; frozen max-drawdown Q75; frozen calendar>=5-day gate. Each binary gate's P_up and P_tail estimated by Laplace (count+1)/(N+2). Online uses previous *matured labels* whereas frozen comparators do not; distinguish information budgets in interpretation. If feasible add a no-lookahead online-vol conditional calibrator only as future separate protocol, NOT this experiment.

## Output and decision
Report per year: N, UP/DOWN counts, >=2% downside count; BA, UP recall, DOWN recall, UP Brier, tail Brier, same-date comparator scores, monthly tail calibration, as-of label maturity assertion and count. Lower Brier better. Hypothesis supported only if tail Brier strictly lower than BOTH frozen constant and frozen vol in **each** 2025 and 2026, without material UP Brier deterioration. No model promotion regardless: 2025/26 repeatedly inspected, overlapping three-session labels; independent 2027 issue-before-outcome prospective qualification with certified vendor vintages and actual bank bid/ask remains mandatory. Do not claim causal alpha or live signal.
