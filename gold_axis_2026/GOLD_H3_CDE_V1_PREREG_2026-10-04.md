## 1. Research question

Can a rare reversal exception be detected when the current origin deviates in several reversal-oriented dimensions from its own historically matched V5-correct continuation twins?

CDE is deliberately distinct from OCS:
- OCS = hourly internal-flow + external lead-lag concurrence.
- CDE = origin-safe counterfactual dislocation relative to matched continuation twins.

No model probability or threshold grid is fit in CDE V1.

- deceleration gap > 0
- opposite-semivariance-share gap > 0

the origin is more exhausted / adverse / option-opposed than otherwise similar historical continuations.
