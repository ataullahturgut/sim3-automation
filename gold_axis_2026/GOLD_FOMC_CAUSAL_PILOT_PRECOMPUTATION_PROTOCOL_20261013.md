# GOLD FOMC POLICY-SURPRISE MECHANISM — DATA GATE & EXPLORATORY PILOT CONTRACT
Date 2026-10-13; branch research-gold-fomc-mechanism-pilot-20261013; base HEAD 4ecdf810e4e76fba37d20b4a94d30d0bc54b30a3. Open PR #118/#119/#120 unmerged. DO NOT train a price-prediction model.
This is a retrospectively motivated scientific exploratory audit, NOT untouched preregistration.
**Data gates**: (1) official Fed 2020–2025 ordinary scheduled statement date/time, not emergency Fed actions, press conferences, minutes, framework speeches, notation votes; (2) M15 XAU quotes from EVduka, exact UTC timestamps at statement t-15 and t+15, plus preceding -60 and follow-on +120; any missing or erroneous endpoints lead to explicit exclusions, not interpolation; (3) USMPD shock statement-window, calendar dates match and flag unscheduled=0; reconstructed future-loaded values may be used only for explanatory event study, NOT an origin-safe pre-release signal; (4) gold available prior state = last **fully completed NY17 session** prior to statement time t, 20-session return and sample SD of 60 completed sessions' daily returns before the t0 gold session; (5) no gold input to shock definition.
**Primary**: full-sample unfiltered ordinary scheduled FOMC statement events, Y = 100 * ln(XAU_mid[t+15]/XAU_mid[t-15]) on exact 15min bar-ending endpoints; exposure = USMPD GSS frozen-before-2016 policy path factor, percent-points (1 pp = 100bp); report bivariate slope, uncertainty, inverse sign sensitivity and 2020/year-one-out. Try no significance if missing variables.
**Secondary**: simple MP1 (short policy) vs GSS path and per-event full table; gold placebo 100 ln(P[t-15]/P[t-60]), leakage/anticipation; gold continuation +15→+120, return distribution; state interactions E × sd60 and E × (r20/(sd60 sqrt20)), only after pre-exposure sample completeness, with HC3 & leave-one-year-out and ill-conditioning diagnostics; small sample/no causal proof. Y continuous; no conditioning on price move selection, no future values as controls.
**Macro inventory gate**: official Fed/BLS repair preliminary identified missing 2016 Jan8 NFP, Jan20 CPI, 2020 Sep4 NFP, 2026 Sep11 CPI, Sep16 FOMC; official source links/timestamps required; archive otherwise incomplete until full independent calendar fetch.
**NY17 audit**: explicit bar-end at 17:00 ET belongs to *session ending that day*; computed label `date((bar_end ET + 7h) - 1 microsecond)` when needed, and check impact. Do NOT alter original authority files.
**Stop**: if fewer than e.g. 25 data-complete scheduled statement events or factor unavailable, produce DATA-ONLY diagnostics and no estimates; otherwise produce clearly provisional descriptive estimates, not verified causal mechanism. No bank fee/backtest on this task.
Sources: https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm ; https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/data-and-indicators/us-monetary-policy-event-study-database/ .
