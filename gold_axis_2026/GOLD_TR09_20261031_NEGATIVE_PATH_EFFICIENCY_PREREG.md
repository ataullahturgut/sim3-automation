# TR09 H3 — negative five-return path efficiency (2026-10-31; preregistration)

**Pre-outcome-scoring research contract.** Test ONE novel hypothesis: a coherent, predominantly negative price path over the previous five *completed* 09:00→09:00 daily BID log returns forecasts future three-eligible-weekday 09:00 DOWN direction and/or a >=2% loss better than frozen controls. Define r1=ln(p1/p0), ..., r5=ln(p5/p4), chronological oldest to newest. Signed path efficiency E=(r1+r2+r3+r4+r5)/(abs(r1)+...+abs(r5)); zero denominator E=0. New gate TRUE iff **E<=-0.50**. This is not a five-day realized-volatility level, volatility acceleration, maximum drawdown, sign-change count, downside semivariance fraction, or largest single-day shock share. Threshold/sign fixed before inspecting outcomes; no posthoc inversion.

**Data and time:** Connected Neon Gold Control project winter-art-94880101, read-only public.gold_research_histdata_xau15m_candidate, native_minute_bars=15, 08:45 Europe/Istanbul bar start and 09:00 nominal completion; six previous-to-origin eligible 09:00 BID closes, future target at third subsequent eligible weekday 09:00 BID close. Same frozen 2026-10-30 holiday exclusions and missing-anchor treatment, outcomes y_up=(pt>p5), y_tail=(pt/p5<=0.98). Historical source archive was backfilled; source first-publication/receipt times, bank executable bid/ask, and 09:00 immediate availability remain UNPROVEN. This is a clock-aligned historical diagnostic, NOT verified live PIT, tradable signal or bank P&L.

**Training and comparisons:** Fit two Laplace (k+1)/(n+2) probabilities (UP and tail) by binary gate on 2023-24 origins only, with target_d<=2024-12-31 purging. 2025 and 2026 Jan-Sep are retrospectively repeatedly examined, not untouched holdouts. Compare on EXACT SAME rows and fixed model definitions: constant, two-UP, five-day realized vol Q75, five-day max-drawdown Q75, calendar >=5, matured60 pooled and matured60 vol-stratified; matured outcomes must have target_d<origin_d. Report N, actual UP/DOWN/tail, BA, UP/DOWN recall, both Brier, per-year subgroup counts; independent SQL timeline audit of target chronology and group outcomes.

**Decision frozen before test:** Reject if either year severe-tail Brier does not beat BOTH vol-Q75 and matured60 vol-strat, or either year BA<52.5%, DOWN recall<20%, or gate TRUE/FALSE group N<30 in either evaluation year. No tuning or inverted rule after outcomes. Even a pass is a research candidate, not live promotion: 2027 prospective pre-registered timestamped issues, independently proven vendor firstprint and executable bank BID/ASK with all-in costs are mandatory. No overwrite of old models, experiments or canonical manifest.
