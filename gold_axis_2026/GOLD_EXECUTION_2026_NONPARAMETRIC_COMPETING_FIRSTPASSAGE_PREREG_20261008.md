# Pre-registration: 2026 triple-outcome competing first-passage analogy
Date 2026-10-08. Research is NOT blind; prior 2026 outcomes already examined.

First passage is defined by FIRST 15-minute BID **closing-price** crossing fixed ±0.50% relative to 17TR BID entry, within regular 17TR→next09TR: outcomes UP_FIRST, DOWN_FIRST, NO_HIT. This is NOT intrabar high/low or actual bank stop/limit execution. No weekend 64h or unobserved market-period gap filling. Use same source-gated observed-close diagnostic from `GOLD_EXECUTION_2026_FIRSTPASSAGE_COMPETING_BARRIER_DIAGNOSTIC_20261008_METRICS.csv`.

Novel, nonparametric, genuinely different target: estimate three mutually exclusive first-passage event probabilities, not endpoint direction. Build 16-bar normalized cumulative four-hour pre-decision trajectory from `gold_execution_2026_full_trajectory_cbr_20261008.py`. Top41 past-matured neighbors only, exponential kernel, class-marginal shrinkage 5 effective prior counts. Candidate `PATH_FIRSTPASSAGE` conditions only on trajectory; `REGIME_PATH_FIRSTPASSAGE` adds 0.30 standardized squared log-pre4h RV and lagged GVZ differences. Baseline `HISTORICAL_THREE_EVENT_PRIOR` is training-sample class probabilities, **not** an old binary HGB miscast as a three-class comparator.

2023–24 past-month refits, 2025 pre-2025 frozen, 2026 pre-2026 frozen, all three labels in training must be fully matured (prior next date<=fit time) and all decision features observed before Turkey 17. Mirror and first-party 2026 populations separate, closed-market padded M1 bars excluded; 2026 source maturity and feature-availability populations must be disclosed. Metrics: 3-class accuracy, macro-average recall, 3-class normalized Brier, multiclass logloss, individual class recalls, paired versus prior average Brier and exact corrected-label delta. Month-by-month 2026 class mix included. NO hyperparameter selection based on 2026 observations. No claim of PnL or brokerage executable barrier orders from mid/BID closes. No promotion without improvement also in 2023, 2024 and 2025, uncertainty and independent untouched prospective outcomes.

Mathematical analogy: competing risks cumulative incidence and cause-specific hazards, though this experiment estimates cumulative first-event probabilities by historical kernel analogue—not a fitted cause-specific survival regression. Triple-barrier labels are well-established (Lopez de Prado; Mathematics 2024 doi 10.3390/math12050780) but **event labels are not portfolio return** (Lima 2026 DOI 10.2139/ssrn.7177958).
