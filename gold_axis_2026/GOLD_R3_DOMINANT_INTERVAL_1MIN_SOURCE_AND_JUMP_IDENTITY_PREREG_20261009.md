## Why

A 15m jump may be a diffusive burst, a reported-quote correction, or several smaller returns. First falsify whether the dominant 15m move is even concentrated in one 1m movement, using a separate licensed research cache; DO NOT claim BNS/LM true jump detection from a single concentration ratio.
## Exact source and clock

Local return interval for r_j is [04:45+j*15m,05:00+j*15m], which ends <=08:45. Join 16 Twelve Data one-minute candle-close observations at timestamps from interval_start MINUS 1 minute through interval_end MINUS 1 minute inclusive; each is an OPEN-TIMESTAMPED 1m bar closing at start+1 minute. This gives 15 consecutive 1m returns.
