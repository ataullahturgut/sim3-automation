## Why V2 is needed

The canonical manifest remained factually current but accumulated new work through append-only checkpoints. After the original model-family consolidation, the project opened several new research tracks:

1. external-variable / feature-information research;
2. ChHHO F0–F4 feature architecture audits;
3. market-regime discovery and live regime detection;
4. transition / extreme / OOD state analysis;
5. multi-signal alarm research and alarm reliability;
6. Specialist Hedge routing;
7. post-alarm rescueability and relative-loss prediction;
8. October-2026 forward execution.

These tracks were scientifically governed but no longer appeared in a single intuitive reading order.

## Separation rules

- **Regime is not alarm.** Regime describes the market state from market-only variables.
- **Alarm is not direction.** Specialist Hedge estimates ChHHO error risk, not UP/DOWN.
- **Alarm is not switch.** Post-alarm rescue is a separate decision problem.
- **Native-input feature augmentation is not residual correction.** Their evidence must remain separate.
- **2025/2026 is opened transport evidence, not selection authority.**
- **Rejected methods remain documented** to prevent accidental reruns.
