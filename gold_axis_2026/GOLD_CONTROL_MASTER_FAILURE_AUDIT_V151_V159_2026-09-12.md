## Purpose

This audit is the stopping-rule document for model proliferation. It does not search for a new winner. It records the repeated failure modes observed after increasingly strict point-in-time, rolling-origin, class-balance, regime and role checks.

## Locked conclusions from V1.51–V1.59

1. A universal normal-day gold direction model is **NOT_PROVEN**.
2. Normal-day H1 direction remains **NO_SIGNAL_UNTIL_SEPARATELY_PROVEN**.
3. H20 RTQ remains the strongest normal-day directional research specialist, but its 2025 one-class behavior forbids unconditional promotion.
4. Employment + Inflation event-time direction remains a separate stronger specialist lane and must not be diluted into a normal-day vote.
5. Monthly price-level forecasting remains a separate target and clock.
6. FAST, SLOW, Monthly Direction, BOCPD, GVZ and Emergency/Reversal are context/risk/regime roles, not equal direction votes.
7. 2025 and 2026 have been researcher-visible repeatedly and cannot be presented as fresh blind proof.
8. Future prospective shadow evidence is required before any production authority.

## Stopping rule

V1.60 is the final normal-day retrospective architecture experiment in this research sequence. It may only test whether two already-existing H20 RTQ experts can be selected or rejected using a transparent, causal, regime-conditioned reliability rule. It may not add a new direction learner, new horizon, new threshold sweep, new feature search, model zoo, ensemble optimization or post-score repair.

After V1.60, the thesis moves to synthesis, prospective shadow collection and writing. A failed V1.60 is a valid result: it implies that normal-day H20 direction is conditional but not sufficiently stable for retrospective promotion under the frozen evidence rules.
