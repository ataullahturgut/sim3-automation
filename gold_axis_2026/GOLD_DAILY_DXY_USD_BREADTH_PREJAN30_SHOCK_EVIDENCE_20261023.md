## As-of governance: Federal Reserve H.10 cannot validate Jan29 as-of inputs

Official Fed 2026-01-26 published H.10: only contains Jan 19-23 rates:
https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h10/20260126/
The Fed H.10 2026-02-02 published Jan 26-30 observations AFTER the Friday Jan30 collapse:
https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h10/20260202/
The Fed states H.10 weekly at Monday 16:15 US ET for *the prior week*. Therefore do not use official H.10 Jan29 or Jan30 rows for a Jan29 EOD or 30 Jan D-1 forecast unless independently earlier market FX quotes and precise timestamps exist. The AmInvest Jan30 09:44 Malaysia published snapshot is timely for Jan29 previous-close facts, not a replacement for a fully point-in-time historical real-time database.

## Actual event chronology

On Jan30, press reports attributed the new USD strength and gold plunge partly to expectations/nomination of Kevin Warsh as Fed chair. The large USD move and the severe precious metals fall appeared within the Jan30 trading session, NOT in Jan29 DXY daily close or the preceding weekly FX breadth.

- Dated Reuters, Jan30: https://www.reuters.com/world/india/gold-slips-firmer-dollar-set-best-month-since-1980-2026-01-30/
- Dated Reuters, Feb2 on Jan30 selloff and CME futures margin increase: https://www.reuters.com/world/china/slump-commodities-rattles-global-markets-2026-02-02/
- Morning Jan30 independent live commentary (published 10:18 GMT), DXY at 05:15 ET approx 96.505 and +0.4% versus Jan29 close: https://uk.investing.com/news/forex-news/dollar-rebounds-on-warsh-talk-still-set-for-weekly-loss-4479316

DXY Jan30 +0.74% at end of day might have warned on continuation risk into Feb2, but original separate D1 ANN did not predict the -7% move: it forecast only -0.26% on Feb2 using Jan30 four-metal inputs. No causal/lead evidence that Jan30 USD rise would on its own predict Feb2 crash of specific magnitude.

## Decision

The tested simple hypothesis **'pre-30-Jan broad dollar strengthening signalled gold crash one day ahead' is NOT supported by pre-event EOD data**.

This does **not** disprove the usefulness of *intraday cross-asset divergence* or an after-shock continuation alert on Jan30; those are separate specifications requiring point-in-time intraday USD and XAU data, event timestamps and pre-2026-trained models and time-ordered evaluation.

No DXY shock-trigger is installed in production, no hypothetical backtested success is claimed, and no Jan30 DXY close is used for Jan30 morning/previous day forecasts. Scores and performance of current gold models remain unchanged.
