## Research implications, not model design
1. **Antecedent vs catalyst vs amplifier** are distinct. Examples: 2025 pre-crash positioning and stretched technicals (antecedent), news/position reduction (catalyst), rapid forced selling and USD squeeze (amplifier).
2. **Event name alone is insufficient**: tariff news accompanies both up and down moves (April 2025); safe haven can invert under liquidity shock.
3. **Relative timestamps determine epistemic validity**: pre-warning 13 Oct 2025 matters, WGC 7 Nov 2023 retrospective and 13 Jun 2024 after June 7 do not constitute lead signals. Publishing a report during a *multiweek* episode does not mean it predicted the episode's start.
4. **Evidence scope**: monthly gold review sources, news explanations, and existing post-release CPI/NFP ledger; they are not a systematic 42-case intraday, point-in-time news exhaustiveness audit. GDELT/Reuters historical feed with provider first-publication timestamps, archived FedWatch expectations, cross-market intraday bars and contemporaneous CFTC/ETF vintages still need event-level join.
5. **Controls essential before inference**: non-event days with same apparent antecedents; repeated co-occurring shocks; placebo timestamps; distinct release surprises as post-release only; multiple-testing and posthoc bias. DO NOT train any predictive model based on this atlas yet.
