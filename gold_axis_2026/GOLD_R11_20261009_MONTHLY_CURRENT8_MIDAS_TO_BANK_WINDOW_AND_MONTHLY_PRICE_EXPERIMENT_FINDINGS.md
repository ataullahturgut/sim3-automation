## 1) What exactly has been tested, no synthetic inputs
Binding original `vw_midas_msvr_successor_v1.py`:
- Same common-dated StakTrakr Au/Ag/Pt/Pd research DAILY series through 2026-07 (no 2026 Aug/Sep daily filled or guessed); monthly p complete, pp=p-1, for target month M p=M-1.
- Monthly mean-metal MR: `MR_m = ln(avg(P_m,p)/avg(P_m,pp))`.
- Exact GPR normalized **historically dated vintage for origin p**, `GPR_z = (GPR_p_vintage[pp] - min(vintage values<=pp))/(max-min)`, each >=24 readings, `available_as_of <=08:45TR/16:45TR` decision-time required. Daily returns in completed month p, `r_i=ln(P_{i+1}/P_i)`. `lambda=0.1exp(-10GPR_z)`, `VW_m=sum(exp(-lambda*age_i)*r_i)/sum(weights)` with newest age0. Eight inputs **AuMR/AuVW, AgMR/AgVW, PtMR/PtVW, PdMR/PdVW**. This IS the original feature-extraction mathematics, not just 1m price trend, no session outcome fed into monthly preprocessing.

History 2022–25 EV Dukascopy archived quote source with stored target label parity, 2026 direct Dukascopy M1-matched complete15min only. Normal Mon–Thu→next weekday OVN, no Friday-to-Monday. Flat target and missing/early GPR months explicitly excluded.
- GPR reconstructed from historical official Git vintage rather than final current vintage, but **historical StakTrakr daily rows' source first-print `available_as_of` is 2026-09-05 because they were backfilled**: timestamp of independent original daily quote publication in 2023–25 **NOT certified**. Treat all results historical as-of feature reconstruction, **not verified broker first-print live execution**. Original GPR revision/archive disclosure remains.

Do not compare 2026 R11 N127 DAY /N91 OVN to earlier R10 N130 DAY/N96 OVN (R10 M1 source extended to October and included different monthly quote-anchor restrictions) or CBR N72 as if same rows.
- Regression learner is a **LOW-CAPACITY translation** of monthly 8 features onto signed bank outcome.
