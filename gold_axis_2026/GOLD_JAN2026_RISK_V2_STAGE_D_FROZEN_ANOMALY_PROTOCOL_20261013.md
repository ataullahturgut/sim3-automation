# STAGE D FROZEN — UNSUPERVISED HIGH-FREQUENCY MARKET DISTRESS STATE (NOT AN EARLY CAUSAL PREDICTION)
Date 2026-10-13. Formed **AFTER** Stage V2 supervised hazard failure (2022–23 training too few severe future events, no 2024/25/Jan alert). This is exploratory methodological selection after Jan outcome already known; 2026 Jan cannot be used as independent validation. Market mechanics no true bid/ask bank logs, no 15min XAG contemporaneous panel; no fabrication.

**Primary goal**: Detect whether a severe *already unfolding* intraday XAU shock is visible while last close-based S4 trend remains positive. Do not equate a detected shock with a forecast of tomorrow's drop or an executable exit.

**Data** `GOLD_JAN2026_RISK_HAZARD_V2_HISTORICAL_VENDOR_SAFE_PANEL_20261013.csv` (factorized EV gold M15 2022–25, HIST gold M15 late 2025/Jan26) and upstream SQL. Completed NY17 sessions >=70 bars. Only as-of t features:
- `D_t = abs(day_ordered_higher_peak_to_later_low_drop_pct)`.
- `W_t = max(0,-100*(close_t/high_t -1))`: the closing quote's distance from the session high.
- `d_base_t = median(D_{t-60}...D_{t-1})` strictly preceding t.
- `w_base_t = median(W_{t-60}...W_{t-1})` strictly preceding t.
- `A_t = sqrt((D_t/d_base_t)^2 + (W_t/w_base_t)^2)`, each denominator floor .1 percentage point; no future features. No ex-post clip.
- Learn **ONE** alert cutoff = empirical 99th percentile (linear interpolation) of A in EV 2022 and 2023 after 60-session warmup; NEVER fit threshold to 2024, 2025 or Jan26. ALERT only if `A_t >= cutoff`. Also record alert vs independent 'next3-session 3% downside event' and false-alert control episodes, but regard as volatility-state anomaly *not event prediction*.
**Portfolio hypothesis**: Add to previously selected S4 long-gold state: if ALERT while holding GOLD, issue SAT after completed session t and fill only next new-session first M15 quote; 5 eligible-session cooldown before re-entry under S4. Unchanged S4 AL r60>=2, SAT r60<=-2, 0.8% each paid action, no spread/slippage, end open gold hypothetical .8% liquidation haircut. DEV alert threshold trained 2022/23; portfolio compare 2024 and 2025 as transfer, NO revised threshold. Required for even exploratory position-risk promotion: 2024 AND 2025 must each improve S4 hypothetical net NAV by >=0.5 percentage point, avoid worse max daily NAV DD by >1pp, <=12 paid actions, at least one true next3day downside event captured without spurious excessive alerts. Otherwise NO_PROMOTION. Jan2026 previously known stress only, regardless of numerical performance. Test source robustness / daylight-hours as separate gate.
**Clock warning:** NY 17EST=01:00 TRT; abnormal signals may first cross inside session before cutoff but any intraday retrospective timestamp requires original M15 bar sequence, not final day's session-high retrospective value. Can't claim Turkey 09–17 executable exit. Return cannot be calculated from intraday low as a fill.
**Output**: train cutoff and 2022/23/24/25/Jan event-level alerts plus false/non-event controls, annual costed S4 and S4+anomaly, Jan stress scores with first-exceedance UTC if valid. No broad causal labels/news firstprints used. No alpha claim from Jan.
