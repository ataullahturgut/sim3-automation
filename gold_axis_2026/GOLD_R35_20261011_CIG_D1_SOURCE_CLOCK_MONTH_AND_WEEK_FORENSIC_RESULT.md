## 3. sqld 104-day source — local midnight-boundary scan, not forecast score

The original Stak `source=sqld` code computes **AVG over UTC-date grouped poll observations**. To validate its **numerical price levels**, independently downloaded **Dukascopy monthly H1 BID/ASK opening-midpoint quotes** were averaged across **24 hours** for different fixed Türkiye daily boundaries. These are equal-hour proxies for source's potentially unequal/raw 15-minute poll average.

**Correct TR–UTC conversion: Istanbul is UTC+03:00 year-round.** TR 00–00 = previous UTC21..currentUTC20, TR 01–01 = previousUTC22..currentUTC21, TR 02–02 = previousUTC23..currentUTC22, **TR 03–03 = currentUTC00..23**, TR 04–04 = currentUTC01..nextUTC00. They are daily *24-hour average-price* windows; **NOT** two executable end-point observations.

## 4. Correct historical %74.40 interpretation after this month/week audit

The 125 frozen historical CIG directions were evaluated versus a **mixed TARGET identity** (not just a time zone). Breakdown: LBMA 18/25, MetalpriceAPI 5/7, StakTrakr 2/4, sqld 68/89. **All original exact 125 predictions and labels retained**.

Same 125 unchanged calls against **one Dukascopy UTC-day hourly mean label**: **87/125=69.60%**; against **one Dukascopy Istanbul 00–00 hourly mean label**: **96/125=76.80%**, **but these are alternative retrospective targets**, **NOT** proof of an original unique 24h point-to-point target; selecting 76.8% on 2026 hindsight would be invalid.

**The actionable experimental question is not which Turkey clock makes the score highest.** It is which **causal, known-at-issue observation target** we want to forecast. A point-to-point 13:30→13:30 (AM fixing) is only consistent for the 29 LBMA provider rows and the model's official 16TR forecast deadline occurs **after** the probable 13:30 benchmark on that same day. The UTC-day average source for later rows includes prices through **03TR next day** and cannot be treated as a 09TR→09TR spot return. There is no legitimate one-clock reinterpretation across Jan–Jul that reproduces all 145 original levels.

**Binding response to user:** The source periods have now been specifically localized: **13:30TR LBMA AM Jan–early Feb**, **daily timestamp ending 03TR next day for MetalpriceAPI history** (release about 03:05TR), **20TR or later Stak spot-seed Feb23–26**, and **03TR→03TR UTC calendar-day mean for sqld Mar–Jul**. The intervals/instants are **not interchangeable**. The original numerical 74.40% is mixed-contract, mixed-vintage retrospective evidence. A future valid D1 target must use **one fixed clock and one causal source history**, with source-ready input audit and predeclared historical backtest. Preserve old models as potential experts/challengers until that specific proper test.
