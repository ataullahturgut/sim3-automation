# R5 — Pre-registered falsification: are our two bank-window direction targets themselves robust to modest execution delay?
**Written BEFORE obtaining R5 label-flip outcomes. 2026-10-09. Author GPT-6. Retrospective study, no trading champion.**

## Scientific question and non-duplication
Existing manifest/R3 and academic root-cause authority show original daily-label CIG failed at 09TR / 17TR, 2026 spot source quote carry-forward, inflated fixed 0.5% barrier hits in a higher vol regime, and bank spreads unknown. But have we ever **matched the same underlying source and dates to test whether a slight realistic ±15min execution-clock displacement changes the signed DAY/OVN target itself**, conditional on economic move size? The old ±30min robustness checks related to a NY17–NY20 timing strategy, NOT these two Istanbul bank windows. This audit is a **diagnosis of metric robustness**, not a new predictive algorithm.

## Data and strict clock
A: 2023–2025 `public.gold_research_evduka_xau_session_target_candidate_v1`, same-source `public.gold_research_evduka_xau15m_bidask_candidate`. Target complete gate mandatory and local adjacent weekday (OVN).
B: 2026 independent retrieval branch `public.gold_research_dukascopy_2026_direct_m1_m15_bidask_v2`, `m1_matched=15`, all matching entry/exit anchors on same exact native source. This is a separate data-production method but SAME Dukascopy upstream; no uninspected 2026 confirmation.
Native M15 bars are timestamped by UTC bar START, have BID CLOSE and ASK CLOSE at start+15min. Convert local `Europe/Istanbul` (UTC+03), not NY local.
For DAY original 09:00→17:00: original anchor closes from bar START 08:45 and16:45; lagged entry 09:15 corresponds bar START09:00; earlier exit16:45 is bar START16:30. Shifts: start-only09:15→17; end-only09→16:45; both09:15→16:45. These shifted-interval labels are measured after event, not features known at 08:45.
For OVN 17:00→next09:00: origin barSTART16:45, end barSTARTnext08:45; later entry17:15 barSTART17:00; early exit next08:45 barSTART next08:30. Shifts: start-only17:15→next09, end-only17→next08:45, both17:15→next08:45. These tests are sensitivity only (user bank typically cannot buy at17:15); NOT new user trading goal.
For all labels compute `log(((bid_close+ask_close)/2)_exit / ((bid_close+ask_close)/2)_entry)`, sign UP >0 else DOWN; strict no zero and no gap filling. Independently verify original anchor sign agrees with governed target `*_y` for A on matching dates; if mismatches occur log them and quarantine, do NOT silently override. For B require both originals and alternatives present and `m1_matched=15`.
Report denominator: full source approved original labels, fully anchor-complete matched, and exact sign flips for each of 3 alternate clocks. Report separately by YEAR+WINDOW and do not pool different cohort comparisons as one score.
Pre-specified magnitude bins according to **original target absolute midpoint return** (post-hoc diagnostic only): <10bp, 10–25bp, 25–50bp, >=50bp. Report n and any-shift flip rate per bin; also original target median |return|. Do NOT claim outcome magnitude bin is known at issue or recommend model trading gate based on it.
Endpoint false confidence test: On paired DAY matching old R3 frozen DIR4 predictions, compare original signed BA with both-shifts BA for same dates and same frozen prediction; DO NOT change any forecast. If severe deterioration for shifted labels, investigate target-clock fragility. This is an evaluation sensitivity, not justified alpha nor evidence actual bank spread.
In 2026 where original endpoint points incomplete, report gate failures; do NOT impute. Any strong sign flips among large ≥50bp outcomes would be notable. Null hypothesis: no clinically relevant change in direction for 15min shifts; numerical conclusion measured, no arbitrary “significant” labeling.
## Decision
Even perfect sign stability does NOT prove predictable direction. Poor sign stability among minor returns would show binary-endpoint classification is sensitive to microstructure and lack of bank cost information, not a software bug. Further forecasting requires real bank BID/ASK and prospectively frozen predictions.
