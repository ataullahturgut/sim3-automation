# Pre-registration: real Bayesian online change-point on competing UP_FIRST/DOWN_FIRST/NO_HIT events (2026-10-08)
**Task:** Estimate the causal probability of the NEXT overnight first-passage competing event at 17TR before night begins. Unlike static per-origin price-shape kNN and boosted direction, this is **sequential Bayesian regime inference over a categorical stochastic process**. All 2026 outcomes have already been inspected; 2026 scores here are *retrospective as-if-live*, NOT untouched prospective.

Evidence and design: Adams & MacKay Bayesian Online Changepoint Detection (BOCPD), arXiv 0710.3742, and applications to order-flow regime changes (Quantitative Finance 2024 doi 10.1080/14697688.2024.2337300); 3-event barrier labels are observed bid **15m-close** barrier ±0.50%, **not** tradable stop-loss execution. This is a *Dirichlet–multinomial BOCPD adaptation* to competing categorical first-event labels, not an implemented continuous-time cause-specific survival-hazard model.

**Fixed Bayesian model:** Dirichlet α=(1,1,1) for UP_FIRST, DOWN_FIRST, NO_HIT (canonical code order NO_HIT,UP_FIRST,DOWN_FIRST); hazard H=1/63 per **observed** eligible overnight session. At each forecast origin, mix posterior predictive three-class probabilities over online posterior **run lengths**; after its result matures by next 09TR, update the run-length posterior using the observed category and the genuine change-point likelihood (Dirichlet prior predictive) versus continuation likelihood (posterior predictive). Truncate run length memory after 252 observed source-qualified sessions for numerical stability, renormalize; state, skipped source dates and matured labels are NEVER overwritten by target-day outcomes.

**Comparators** on EXACT same 2023–24 monthly chronological, 2025/2026 retrospective 3-class feature/label-qualified samples: (A) last-calendar-year fixed three-event climatology, (B) previous 63 observed matured-event frequencies with Dirichlet pseudocount1 each, and (C) previously frozen `REGIME_PATH_FIRSTPASSAGE` nonparametric geometric analogue, without its 2026 labels in fit. Outcome metrics: 3-way Brier (lower better), logloss, classwise/macro recall, actual alert proportion for down-first, 2026 paired Brier differences on same dates and 1500 month-block bootstrap CI, all reported 2023–26. No label edits or cherry-picked times; source populations native direct and third-party Dukascopy-upstream mirror separate and overlapping (not independent).

**Success gate:** evidence of improvement over both 63-event trailing Dirichlet and last-year climatology in 2025 and 2026, no sharp 2023/24 collapse, and nontrivial DOWN_FIRST sensitivity/conditional event-risk calibration. Given inspected 2026, no outcome can authorize a live champion absent prospective later year and actual bank execution contract.

**Caveat:** This is a model of event probabilities, NOT a model for causal causes of Fed news, risk-neutral spot option pricing, or bank-executable trading profits. BOCPD is genuine structural regime-state modeling, distinct from class-weight reweighting of a binary classifier.
