# 2026-10-09 prereg: Nightly HAR-X volatility risk forecast on actual 17TR to next09TR path

Scientific authority: Fulvio Corsi (2009) A Simple Approximate Long-Memory Model of Realized Volatility, Journal of Financial Econometrics, DOI 10.1093/jjfinec/nbp001; Engle (1982) ARCH, Econometrica; Gibbs/Candès (2021) adaptive forecasting under shifts. HAR-RV is risk magnitude, **NOT** direction; no income return, no bank spread PnL.

Challenge: 2026 fixed 0.5% first-passage incidence jumps 2023 55.56% to2026 95.28%, yet volatility-normalized label approximately 51.5% in2023 and44.9% in2026. Strong evidence changes in volatility scale affect labels. A signal-power study needs a conditional volatility layer BEFORE using signed first-passage risk.

Exact ex-ante target: normal weekday 17TR→next09TR realized overnight RV = sqrt(sum((log(BID M15close/M15open))² over exactly64 complete M15 bars)) in LOG units. Reject any missing source bar or weekend long horizon. Futures market data not needed; two 2026 Dukascopy prices evaluated separately, original 2020–25 EV Dukascopy source unchanged. Features at 17TR only: log(preceding last completed overnight RV), log(last5 completed nightly RV mean), log(last21 completed nightly RV mean), log(last4h 16 complete M15 pre17 RV). Require preceding21 source-qualified normal overnight RV, 21-case history span<=65 calendar days, after which audit source missing instead of silently stale values. If insufficient data abstain. No future same-date label, macro, or price observed after 17 in features.

Freeze SINGLE HAR-X ridge estimator: StandardScaler()+Ridge(alpha=10.0), squared-log-RV target log(actual RV), fixed 4 feature names, no 2026 tuning or feature screening. Model 2023–24 month-start refit past fully matured source-qualified nights, 2025 fit pre-Jan1 2025, 2026 fit pre-Jan1 2026; 2026 eligible after 2025 fit never included. Minimum historic fit100 labels (after first21 RV features). **Strong fair on-exact-dates** baselines: previous21 night arithmetic mean RV, previous5 mean RV, and HAR-three classic (Ridge alpha10 using prior1/5/21 logs without 4h pre17). Report RV MAE in percentage points, mean absolute percentage error (MAPE when y>0), root mean square percentage error, QLIKE on RV²: log(Vhat/V)+V/Vhat-1 (lower better) with hard clipping positivity, paired 2025 and2026 source-split error differential (500 month-block bootstrap fixed seed20261009), action-ready risk >= historical expected volatility threshold (source prior 21 only). QLIKE is VERY sensitive to sparse outliers; also report median ratio. Strictly no claim HAR-RV is signed UP/DOWN prediction; require HAR-X vs HAR-three AND 21-day naive improvements consistently in 2025 and2026, 2023/24 no catastrophic failure before any favorable risk-channel designation. Source mirror/direct 2026 overlap, not independent.
