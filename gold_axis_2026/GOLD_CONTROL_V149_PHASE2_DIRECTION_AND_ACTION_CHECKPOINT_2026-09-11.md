## Decision

The proposed role-preserving, event-aware and regime-conditioned direction is scientifically eligible **as a preregistered research hypothesis**, not as a final model or an equal-vote ensemble.

Two separate studies are required:

1. A general `NEXT_NY17_1D` / `NEXT_NY17_3D` incremental-information programme.
2. A sparse event-time `MACRO_EVENT_SUCCESSOR_V2 + MARKET_SHOCK` study.

They must not be merged into one evaluation clock. A post-event confirmation cannot be used at an origin that precedes the confirmation. Macro actual values released at 08:30 New York may only enter origins after that release.
