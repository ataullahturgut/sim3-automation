## 1. Binding conclusion

The current tactical problem is not simply “transaction costs are too high.”

The frozen Stage-6 contract links:
- assumed round-trip cost `c`
- and entry hurdle `U > c`.

As a result, economic performance is **non-monotonic** in cost because a higher assumed cost also suppresses marginal trades.

Therefore the Stage-6B result must be read as **cost/hurdle feasibility bands**, not as a simple universal break-even ceiling.

The cleanest current cost/hurdle result is:

> **U3 PROB_TILT passes the full tactical gate only from 0 to 1.25 bp round-trip.**

This is the only broad, economically intuitive low-cost PASS band.

U2 produces a narrow 23–24 bp PASS region, but that is generated by the higher cost simultaneously acting as a stronger entry filter. It must **not** be interpreted as evidence that expensive execution is desirable.

No tradable instrument is frozen yet because cross-instrument target tracking has not been empirically audited.

2025 remains closed.
