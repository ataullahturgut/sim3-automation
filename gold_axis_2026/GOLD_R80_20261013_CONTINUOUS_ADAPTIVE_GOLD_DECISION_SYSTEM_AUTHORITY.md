# R80 — Continuous online gold decision system (LIVE SHADOW FIRST)
Created 2026-10-13, branch independent of research PRs #113–#116; NO canonical merge, NO bank orders.

## User-defined economic purpose
Use existing monthly point-price trend for core allocation; continuously process observed intraday onset AND ahead-of-onset forecast signals; decide whether to temporarily switch GOLD↔CASH after the move starts or before it and when to re-enter after reversal/stabilization. The action vocabulary is **AL, SAT, BEKLE, YENIDEN_GIR** (alerts/advice only; not bank API trade commands). Separate risk and direction and expose uncertainty and pending, never forced trade.

## Architecture: real streaming services required
1. **Ingestion**: independently source-timestamped XAU spot BID/ASK, bank executable ASK/BID (bank SELL price to us for buys / bank BUY price for our sells), plus XAG, USD index, rates, CME NQ/ZN/CL, scheduled Fed/CPI/PCE/NFP and timestamped surprise/consensus releases. Each record has event_time_utc, received_at_utc, provider, bar state / bar_start / bar_end and immutable source fingerprint. Missing = unknown (never synthetic, never quote interpolation).
2. **Always-on filters**: fast/slow adaptive state-space level, causal wavelet/EMD only with past bars, trend slope & CUSUM sequential move-onset, volatility multiscale (1m,5m,15m,1h, DAY HAR-CAL only for same-window realized variance), cross-metal relative value, cross-market returns, macro news event-time phases. Each model publishes forecast origin, horizon, target semantics, issue expiry, confidence calibration and valid data sources. Prevent multi-model correlated voting inflation.
3. **Three predictive heads**: (i) pre-onset extreme move hazard UP/DOWN, (ii) after-onset continuation or reversal and residual extent over 30/60/120m and until user 16:30 trade cutoff, (iii) stabilization/re-entry hazard. Risk head separate for adverse downside. Fast regime change alarm (drift of residual/calibration) can revoke model rather than reweight based on target-day leakage.
4. **Controller and risk gate**: core monthly position from issue-frozen monthly outlook; validated action-specific expected net value vs BEKLE after 2-sided bank spread, commissions and regret; false exits and missed rallies counted. SELL overlay only if effective additional downside risk demonstrably outweighs cost and lost upside; BUY overlay only if remaining upside after trigger still offsets costs. Hysteresis and cooldown; max trades/day; no leverage and never use future risk realized at decision. Four action outputs with UNKNOWN/NO_TRADE reasons. State and basis ledger prevents fake sell when holding none.
5. **Learning**: event log with immutable forecast (no rewriting), delayed label maturation at exact specific output target end, online recalibration or controlled online Bayesian update ONLY from matured labels, time-decayed model weights and drift detectors, periodic retraining with frozen train/holdout and rollback. Every decision must be replayable by asof source and reproducible with feature snapshots. Multiple test comparisons adjusted for repeated variants.
6. **UI/notification**: continuously updating observed XAU and gold-holding state, signal onset strength, p(adverse continuation), p(reversal), p(stabilization) for stated horizon, economic edge after bank spread, ETA until bank window ends, GREEN when data/feedback/forecasts actually validated and executable; otherwise OBSERVE_ONLY. Notify only on meaningful state changes; no constant BUY/SELL spam.
7. **Runtime**: persistent worker/WebSocket (or scheduled pull with verified acquisition times, rate limits, health checks); Neon append-only history read/write ONLY on approved isolated schema after operator approval. GitHub Actions can replay/test/build, but is **not** a precise subminute continuous market stream service. ChatGPT automations min hourly; NOT fit for 15m/1m trade notifications. No tool connection to the user's actual bank for orders.

## Immediate prototype semantics
`tools/gold_r80_paper_live_watch.py` is a **pure standard-library, deterministic replay** of received spot quote updates into observed-onset WATCH states. It reports `BEKLE` and reason if no approved predictive probabilities, monthly-asof forecast, bank bid/ask, or quote-age evidence. It deliberately contains NO magic "right now BUY/SELL" fabricated model. The code *can process events as they arrive from a future adapter*, but none is currently connected.
No real trades, no prompt-based hallucinated prices, no model retraining on future or current labels.
