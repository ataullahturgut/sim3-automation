# TR09 H3 — five-session absolute-return shock concentration: preregistration (2026-10-29)

**Freeze before any outcome scoring.** New limited hypothesis: the most extreme absolute return among the last five completed 09:00-to-09:00 XAU/USD returns accounts for **at least 50%** of their total absolute variation. This "one-day shock concentration" could change the next-three-eligible-weekday 09:00 direction and probability of a price decline >=2%. Binary gate: `max(|r1|,...,|r5|)/sum(|r1|,...,|r5|)>=0.50`; zero denominator => FALSE. This is NOT the earlier 15-minute single-minute burst concentration, the lagged5 realized-volatility Q75, the downside-semivariance share, max drawdown, or the 5-return sign alternation flag. No threshold tuning after results.

**Source and causal clock:** Read-only Neon project `winter-art-94880101`, `public.gold_research_histdata_xau15m_candidate`. HistData M1-derived M15 BID close for the completed 08:45–09:00 Europe/Istanbul interval; require native_minute_bars=15, all five prior and current anchors, and target. Reuse **exact** fixed eligible-weekday calendar, 2023–24 target-date-purged training and 2025 / 2026 Jan–Sep retrospective cohort from the 2026-10-28 SQL. Label: third next eligible weekday 09:00 BID anchor versus origin anchor, UP if >0, severe DOWN if <=-2%. Historical provider firstprint, zero-latency 09:00 origin readiness and executable bank ASK/BID are **unproven**; never claim bank-executable 09:00 signal. The H3 labels overlap; 2025/2026 were repeatedly inspected, not independent confirmation.
