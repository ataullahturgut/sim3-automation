## 3. Empirical motivation from the 2023-2025 15-minute XAU/USD study

The 73,891-bar Türkiye-time diagnostic showed:

- 17:00-18:00: 10.87% of realized-variance share;
- 16:00-17:00: 10.55%;
- 15:00-16:00: 7.41%;
- 18:00-19:00: 7.08%;
- 04:00-05:00: 5.51%.

09:00->17:00 and 17:00->next 09:00 are both economically material. Overnight cannot be discarded:

- mean absolute move: about 0.44% vs 0.51%;
- 90th percentile absolute move: about 0.95% vs 1.15%;
- |move| >= 1%: about 9.1% vs 11.6% of days;
- overnight absolute movement exceeds daytime movement on about 53.5% of days.

The daytime direction does not provide a sufficiently strong deterministic continuation rule for overnight. A separate overnight target is therefore justified.

## 5. Literature-backed hybrid direction

### 5.1 Gold-specific market-structure authority

Gold price discovery is intraday and state dependent. Hauptfleisch, Putnins & Lucey (Journal of Futures Markets, 2016) find that London spot and New York futures both contribute to price discovery, with New York futures larger on average and strong intraday/time-varying effects.

Sobti, Sehgal & Ilango (International Review of Financial Analysis, 2021) find that:
- US futures lead round-the-clock gold price discovery;
- the New York/London overlap is the most informative time zone;
- US and Eurozone macro surprise effects are state dependent and can be asymmetric/sign-reversing.

This supports preserving session identities as expert/state information even when final labels are execution aligned.

### 5.2 Forecast-combination authority

Forecast-combination literature supports combining **accurate and diverse** forecasts rather than simply increasing model count.

Samuels & Sekkel (International Journal of Forecasting, 2017) show gains from trimming model pools using out-of-sample performance before averaging.

Felici & Sudoso (Annals of Operations Research, 2026) jointly optimize model selection and forecast weights and explicitly penalize redundant/correlated forecasts; their results support diversity-aware combination rather than equal voting among similar forecasters.

The Handbook of Economic Forecasting forecast-combination literature also emphasizes that combination gains depend on error variance, correlation and instability; complex optimal weights can fail when the model pool is large relative to available history.

### 5.3 Dynamic / regime-aware authority

Recent financial/time-series work supports adaptive expert routing under non-stationarity:

- Hybrid Recurrent Expert Gating (Procedia Computer Science, 2026): adaptive softmax gating reallocates weight among experts under changing financial regimes.
- Non-stationarity in financial time series survey (Neurocomputing, 2026): maintains multiple specialized models and adapts model-level selection/weights as regimes evolve.
- Dynamic feature selection with distributional Markov error correction (Applied Soft Computing, 2026): combines adaptive feature selection, state transitions and error correction under regime-dependent financial dynamics.

These studies support a **low-capacity, soft-gated, competence-aware expert system** for the current sample rather than an immediate large Transformer/LSTM stack.

## 7. Recommended hybrid research sequence

### Hybrid-0 — diversity-trimmed static benchmark

Before any adaptive gate:
- cluster mechanically/nested highly correlated experts;
- retain one representative per redundant family unless a variant proves distinct incremental error;
- compare simple equal-weight/median and development-trimmed combinations;
- no 2025/2026-driven membership selection.

Purpose: establish whether diversity alone already improves the weak single-model baseline.

### Hybrid-1 — Execution-Clock Competence Gate (ECCG)

Primary research candidate.

Architecture:
- separate gate for DAY and OVERNIGHT;
- experts remain independently trained/frozen;
- gate sees only information available at the execution origin;
- inputs may include expert probabilities, disagreement, recent matured same-target competence, volatility/path state, session state and known event-calendar state;
- use **soft weights**, not hard winner-take-all routing, as the first specification;
- constrain model capacity because the effective number of daily target labels is much smaller than the raw 15-minute bar count.

Purpose:
- learn *which expert is trustworthy now*, not relearn the whole market from scratch.

### Hybrid-2 — Residual / rescue router

Second research candidate, descended from DPTC/RTE logic.

Architecture:
- start from the best balanced execution-window base;
- specialists do not vote continuously;
- a correction layer activates only under preregistered disagreement/error-state conditions;
- evaluate **net rescues minus newly broken calls**, not standalone accuracy.

Purpose:
- exploit reversal/correction specialists without allowing them to damage normal-regime calls.

### Hybrid-3 — change-point / regime handoff

Only after sufficient target-specific matured history exists.

Candidate concepts:
- BOCPD-style change probability;
- competence decay/hysteresis;
- transition-state RTE/STCR-style controller.

Purpose:
- reduce weight on stale experts during structural breaks.

This stage must remain blocked if the execution-target history is too short to estimate competence safely.

Reason: current evidence indicates the main problem is **target alignment + expert competence heterogeneity**, not lack of raw model capacity.
