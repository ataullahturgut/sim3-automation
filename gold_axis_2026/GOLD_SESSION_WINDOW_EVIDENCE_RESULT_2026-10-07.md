## Governance and comparison rules

Development = 2023–2024, with inherited governed 2022 warm-up where required. 2025 is archived transport only. No 2026 rows, random split or target-window features enter role discovery. Every repair block selects only from labels whose `end_utc <= first test origin`, and every train origin is earlier. Inherited upstream raw producer source-ready checks remain in force.

The primary table ranks native-development BA among N≥80 candidates with both recalls≥30%, retaining the inherited sample/class-collapse safety convention. This is a descriptive screening rule, not a significance or membership gate. Native coverage differs substantially. Pairwise results additionally report each BA/Brier on the exact same timestamps; a native leader is not asserted to dominate on an identical population. Full and selected same-family variants are dependent representations, not independent future votes.

The two-year flag asks N≥80 in each year and BA>50% with both recalls≥30%. It is descriptive, not proof of significance. The BA intervals are ordinary approximate class-recall intervals without serial-dependence or multiplicity correction. Rankings search an existing inventory and therefore cannot establish confirmatory statistical edge. **No window is labelled proven unpredictable in principle; NO ROBUST EDGE means this inventory has not established stable generalizable edge.**

## Failure mechanisms and restrained innovation

[GOLD_SESSION_WINDOW_EVIDENCE_FAILURE_MECHANISMS_2026-10-07.csv](GOLD_SESSION_WINDOW_EVIDENCE_FAILURE_MECHANISMS_2026-10-07.csv) decomposes the development leader by origin-known preceding 12-hour return and realized volatility. Raw 15-minute bars become available after their close and only strictly prior completed bars are used; both 12-hour anchors must be within 60 minutes, so weekend/session gaps are excluded; no target-window path enters a feature. RV high/low uses the 2023 median diagnostic boundary, not a tuned 2025 threshold. These subgroup diagnostics are descriptive and were not used to choose a feature, gate or model role. Continuation/reversal outcome columns are explicitly `OUTCOME_ONLY`; they cannot be routed at origin.

Asia Afternoon structural evidence is asymmetric: preceding-UP BA 59.17% (N=113) versus preceding-DOWN 42.99% (N=77). Sobti Europe S17 has high-RV BA 55.17% (N=226) versus low-RV 50.41% (N=113). This points to state dependence/class bias rather than a universal session classifier. Per-year breakdown is retained to expose sparse subgroups and regime changes. No macro-event state is invented without a governed origin-ready event source.

A possible later development-only challenger would distinguish continuation from reversal using preceding path/RV and existing structural-versus-path disagreement. It would target the asymmetric errors above, use only origin-known raw features, require nested chronology (no outcome-defined gates), and face severe sample-size/multiple-search risk. Its expected different errors would arise from conditioning structural evidence on prior path state rather than duplicating another linear full-panel vote. **No such challenger was tested or selected here; no new model family was added.**

## Exact next stage

The evidence map is complete as a descriptive and negative-evidence inventory. A deployment-ready predictor has not been established. The next scientific step is a preregistered development-only mechanism challenge with minimum subgroup support and nested causal selection, or an untouched future-period confirmation of a locked candidate. In particular, a nested selected policy needs a new untouched confirmation period; reopening already-seen 2025 to choose policy, threshold or role would not be a fresh validation.

Window-specific consensus, diversity trimming, SENTRY/DART/AIM/AURORA/HELIOS, ECCG, BOCPD/DPTC/RTE-family and final fixed-Istanbul execution router remain **deferred**, with no automatic authorization to start them. Old H3 models are preserved as lineage/future context experts, never transferred directly as session labels.
