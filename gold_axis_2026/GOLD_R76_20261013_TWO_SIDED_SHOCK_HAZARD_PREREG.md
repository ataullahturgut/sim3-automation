# R76 — Two-sided XAU shock alert and re-entry research preregistration
Date: 2026-10-13. **PRE OUTCOME / PRE SCORING.**
Goal: not ordinary every-day UP/DOWN. User has a separate **monthly** XAU regime forecast, wants timely **short correction exits during UP months**, and opportunistic rebound entries during DOWN months; 09:00–17:00 TR is executable bank decision window. Monthly forecast is only used if archived as-of issue timestamp exists; never use realized month return/HMM labels for ex-ante signal.

## Target and horizon
Research baseline issue **09:00 Europe/Istanbul** on weekday t, midpoint quote from source 09:00 M15 OPEN, *one vendor per forecast path*. Event horizon: until same 09:00 clock on the **third following available Mon–Fri price-date** (3 consecutive eligible 09 anchors; weekend calendar time disclosed). Strictly future M15 bars from 09:00 issue through but excluding target 09:00 bar.
Fixed barriers relative 09 midpoint: DOWN <=-1.5%, UP >=+1.5% as soon as bid/ask midpoint M15 LOW/HIGH reaches barrier. Classes: DOWN_FIRST; UP_FIRST; NONE. Ties (upper and lower threshold within same 15m bar) = AMBIGUOUS and excluded from directional winner but count ANY_SHOCK yes. Record if both barriers reached (order important), timing and max adverse excursion. No 'daily close price direction' substitution.
**Primary outcome = two binary competing event risks** DOWN_FIRST and UP_FIRST, and ANY_SHOCK for calibration; primary score Brier plus event-ranked AUROC and top20% lift/precision versus rolling prior event rate, weighted/separated by year. First barrier captures transient corrections missed by endpoint H3 D0→D3, distinct from old R18–R29 3-day endpoint >=1.5% DOWN.
Secondary 1 trading session barrier ±1.0% from 09 next eligible09; and stability/no-shock after a realized event, as descriptive only (not gate-fitted from opened 2025/26).

## Availability and data gates
Neon read-only source 2020–25 EV/Dukascopy-derived M15 BID/ASK (audited 141890 rows); 2026 direct Dukascopy M1-to-M15, `m1_matched=15` only. Contract 09 midpoint OPEN, future highs/lows midpoint high/low proxy not executable bank asks/bids; if both +/- touched same bar, mark ambiguous. Pre-origin features solely **completed** bars ending <=09:00 and past 09:00 price anchors. Gate >=14 of expected16 preissue M15 bars (05:00–09:00 TR) and source-complete 3-day outcome path (>=~70 future M15 per each active source day, no >60 min intraday gap) and no target crossed vendor boundary. No missing-price interpolation; exclude invalid rows with explicit reasons. Fri→Mon/weekend gap flagged.
Risk/safety: bank transaction ASK/BID source unknown, so never assert PnL/executable model. Entry/exit/risk-off state machine proposal requires transaction cost and real bank quotes, must not trade automatically.

## Frozen lean predictors and models
Inputs available at t: last completed 4h gold preissue return; square-root 4h RV; last 5 and20 matured 09→09 returns; trailing20 std of 09→09 returns; signed last-day return; preorigin 4h negative vs positive semivariance. All features use past prices only, never same-day late bar, postissue high, future month label.
Baselines: expanding prior class frequencies, trailing60 matured event frequencies, simple trailing20 absolute 09 return volatility monotone high-risk score; trend-direction-only risk (past5d sign). These are not refitted on test labels.
Challenger 1: 2 low-capacity regularized logistic event probabilities (UP_FIRST, DOWN_FIRST), predictors z-scored on prior data only; frozen L2 alpha=5, intercept unpenalized. Challenger 2: logistic ANY_SHOCK (same).
Fit chronology: 2020–22 warmup/training (all outcomes mature), rolling refit yearly: 2023 trained 2020–22; 2024 trained through 2023; **2025/26 fixed 2020–24** training if possible, no 2025/26 risk outcome used to choose feature, coefficient, barrier, threshold. Min 350 matured prior origins. No class reweighting, no undersampling. Sensitivity month-by-month descriptive, not training. Source-vendor shift 2026 declared.
Hard score gate: challenger Brier< rolling60 baseline and event AUC>0.5 in **both** 2023 and 2024 and nonnegative top20 precision lift vs historical conditional rolling60 frequency in **both** 2025 and 2026, **with blocks/bootstrap CI excluding no improvement** on 2023–24; otherwise REJECT/EXPLORATORY only. Do not select thresholds or modify barriers by seeing 2026 outcomes.
Evaluate prevalence and class rank, top-quintile confidence, UP/DOWN separately, actual % max/min excursion, first-hit time, separations of events that reverted by t+3 09 and which original endpoint sign misses.
Extension only after measured stage1: reconstruct time-valid monthly forecast issue and target, combine shock risk with monthly position state and maturity-correct bank execution. Stability entry = conservative risk decays; 3-day no-shock ground truth is not origin-ready and never a feature at t. Compare monthly buy/hold vs monthly+shock hold/cash at actual bank 09/16:45 source prices with round-trip 10/25/50bp scenarios *only after* model passes conditional risk tests. Include cost of false exits/missed rebound.
## Existing experiments not to misreport or repeat
R20–R29 already forecast severe 3-day **endpoint DOWN >=1.5%**, GVZ and pre-open CME features with inconsistent 2023–26 transport. R75 confidence and prior-path gating fails 2025–26. R69 H3 exact-clock entry generally fails bank-cost validation. R76 targets **first touch of either barrier with transient shock distinction**, not another arbitrary endpoint DOWN label. Do not claim novel predictable edge until tested.
