## A. STRONGER NEW RESULT: PSEUDOREPLICATION AUDIT OF 'STRETCHED PRICE'
V3 originally found 74 daily exposure observations with previous 20 completed NY17 trading sessions >=+8%. Future 10-session CLOSE first passage hits -3% on 21/74 observations (28.4%), >=+3% on 33/74 (44.6%). However these observations are **not 74 independent advance warnings**.
Taking only each new first crossing of >=8% from prior day's <8%, WITH all subsequent ten-session endpoints observed, yields only **13** first-crossing observations (some from same ongoing rally episode):
- 2023: 4 first-crossings, 1 future DOWN, 0 future UP;
- 2024: 4 first-crossings, 0 future DOWN, 2 future UP;
- 2025: 5 first-crossings, 1 future DOWN, 2 future UP.
Pooled 13: **DOWN 2/13**, **UP 4/13**, **neither 7/13**.
Matched each of 13 dates to one other date **within the same calendar year and nearest previous-60-day gold return SD**, constrained previous 20-day return <5%, no reuse, >10 trading sessions from its risk onset, and controls outside +/-5 trading sessions of ALL previous-20-day >=8% dates. Matches **13/13**. Outcomes among controls: **DOWN 1/13**, **UP 8/13**, **neither 4/13**. These are exploratory, not independently randomized and not comparable risk probabilities: controls chosen retrospectively and no formal causal matching diagnostics, their future windows also overlap in places. Matching is **strong on prior volatility, weak on other exposures** (e.g. overnight macro/gold trend and recent return). The samples of 13 are too small.
Interpretation: the previously attractive 21/74 downside frequency is potentially a product of closely overlapping exposure days and high-volatility regime. Data do NOT support launching a sell signal.
All rows in `GOLD_EVENT_CHAIN_2023_2025_V4_13_TENSION_ONSETS_MATCHED_CONTROLS_20261013.csv`. Complementary risk-set V3 all-774-day controls retained. Need outcome-blind dated event sampling and fresh years for credible calibration.

## C. IMPORTANT EVIDENCE DISCRIMINATION
- Same Reuters news story may be published/updated multiple times; where first publication **minute** is unavailable, classify only by DATE, never pretend to have exact NY17 or UTC article firstprint.
- The futures-vs-spot $40 premium refers to **different contract/venues**; it is NOT arbitrage-free guaranteed profit, nor the investor's executable bank spread.
- V4 matched controls are only same YEAR and prior 60-session gold SD; not controls matched on Fed/policy, prior r5, cross-asset illiquidity or structural gold demand. Outcome-biased threshold chosen after historical inspection.
- WGC gold price dates and XAU's NY17 bid/ask-mid represent different observable price constructs, do not splice intraday print points.
- 2024 Aug sudden liquidity crash and January 2025 slow EFP rally are evidence for **separate event ontology**: funding/market plumbing and physical-location dislocation. Scheduled macro calendar alone isn't complete.
- Absence of time-stamped native DXY, DFII10, daily ETF, PIT CFTC, FedWatch 2023–25 means no quantitative causal contributions were attributed to these feeds.
- Good PRIOR research: 2023–25 event date windows; same-source price changes and ranges; exact dated research/blog/publication chronology. Still missing: at-source first-print timestamps, pretrend controls matched to relevant policy/liquidity regimes, unadjusted option/warehouse flow data with vintage, replicable event-level intra-session trigger horizon.

## D. STATUS / NEXT
Findings: Overstretched price is at best a broad vulnerability state, not a forecasted SELL condition; contemporaneous flow and liquidity/event context sometimes determines sign. The strongest new source-documented **non-macro** channels are Aug 2024 funding deleverage and Jan 2025 EFP/London-vs-US physical transfer. 2024 March supplies an important continuing-UP countercase, April 2025 counterexample to static risk-off rule.
No models created, no live alarms, no P&L or bank execution simulation; 2026 cannot be claimed untouched prospective test.
