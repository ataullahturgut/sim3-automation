## Statistical price-analogue comparator, not a causal event-DNA model

1. Features computed from last **10 completed comparable daily closes**: log returns `r1 = log(C[t]/C[t-1])`, `r3`, `r5`, `r10`; `dd5 = log(C[t]/max(C[t-4],..C[t]))`. All features same market-price source; calendar gaps of more than 20 days in trailing 10 valid closes make a row invalid.
2. Historical labels `future5 = log(C[t+5]/C[t])` require no gap > 12 calendar days between source and fifth later valid closing session. No 2026 target in training.
3. Each feature is normalized by **standard deviation measured only across valid 2023–25 training rows**. Euclidean distance among standardized five features; deterministic nearest-neighbor selection sorted by (distance, date ascending). To reduce overlaps, retain neighbors at least **5 calendar sessions apart** in original training row index; request **21 independent-ish neighbors**. If fewer than 21, signal `ABSTAIN`.
4. Prediction = historical **median** of selected five-close-ahead log returns; positive-sign share = count(future5>0)/21 and negative-sign share count(future5<0)/21. Require both `median_future5 > log(1/(0.992*0.992))` and `p_up >= 0.65` to suggest BUY, or both `median_future5 < -log(1/(0.992*0.992))` and `p_down >= 0.65` to suggest SELL when holding gold. Otherwise HOLD existing position. This **21/65%** threshold is chosen as a conservative sample/confidence rule ahead of the simulation, **not estimated from January profitability**. It may never trade. Use the algebraic price cost of two fee sides rather than the retrospectively fitted 2%/3% barriers.
5. When there is a price candidate, apply independent **DNA safety permission gate**: H-UP01 requires **as-of firstprint macro content AND synchronized rate/USD pathway AND credible remaining move**, H-DN01 similar for hawkish channel, other ORG patterns require their specific *independent* identifying evidence (e.g., gold versus synchronized white metals, funding). If these data are not auditable as-of, `DNA_PERMISSION=NO` and **NO automatic BUY/SELL**. A price candidate can still be reported as a *separate diagnostic comparator*.
6. **No price-only candidate is promoted to validated economic DNA.** Two portfolios are tracked separately: `DNA_STRICT` (only permitted economic trades) and `PRICE_ANALOG_DIAGNOSTIC` (pure price-analogue rule, deliberately not marketed as a DNA system). Both start with 100 USD **CASH**. Always compare against CASH, and Jan early buy/hold benchmark separately.

## January execution only after freeze

- Scoring source: January 2026 M15 HistData at **21:45 UTC**, daily decision after bar completion **22:00 UTC**. Weekend or holiday without a confirmed bar has no new signal. If no price or no stable ten-close history, `ABSTAIN/HOLD` as appropriate. January data are loaded only after this document has been committed.
- Execution for a signal: **next eligible UTC 09:00 M15 open**, **only if an actual Dukascopy direct bid_open (SELL) / ask_open (BUY) is recorded**. If no direct quote, mark `EXECUTION_BLOCKED_NO_SOURCE`, keep position; no invented bid/ask from HistData. Apply `0.992` at each side. If a signal arrives when 09:00 of following date is unavailable, check successive valid 09:00 UTC slots and require any conflicting new position change signals at intervening days be audited.
- No position shorting, no leverage, cash zero interest. January 31 is a Saturday; terminal mark uses **last available January 30 executable bid close** when gold is held, with a final-sale 0.8% liquidation-cost provision. Do not use February prices or Feb 2 to score January. If no end bid is available report separate `unrealized_proxy` rather than invent a price.
- Baselines: $100 cash; single buy at first available Jan 2 09:00 ASK and at month end liquidation BID, fees, with source/missing data clearly stated.
- For each of 31 calendar days log date, source quality, last permissible historical closes, feature vector, `neighbor_ids`/distance and `median_future5`, price-only signal, timing/independent economic evidence/ORG rival, strict permission, both separate portfolios and order timing. Do **not** feed a day's next prices into features.
- After completion, store a separate OLAY-02 daily ledger and results; keep prior invalidated January report intact. ÖĞREN-03 receives no date/price/outcome leakage. Annual report/ANA only link the outcome with honest classification.

**Falsification/research limits:** Training price bars are reconstructed, not broker point-in-time feeds; neighboring historical training rows can overlap in time even with spacing, dated press articles may be updated after first publication, and the analyst already knows January results. Thus the resulting price-model gains/losses are a **temporal-input-valid reconstruction on a contaminated evaluation month**, not independent blind forecasting evidence. Using 2023–25 past history as a price-only comparator does not prove economic event DNA.
